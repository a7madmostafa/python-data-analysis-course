The data

About 124,000 job postings published on LinkedIn in the US over four weeks of spring 2024. Each posting has a title, company, location, work type, experience level, salary (sometimes), remote flag, views and applications.

Extra tables link each posting to its job functions (Sales, IT, Management… LinkedIn calls them "skills"), its industries, its benefits, and to company information.

About the format

  • The download is a zip of several folders: postings.csv at the top, then jobs/ (job_skills, job_industries, salaries, benefits), mappings/ (skills, industries) and companies/.
  • postings.csv is ~500 MB, mostly because of two long text columns (description, skills_desc). If your laptop struggles, read without them with usecols.
  • Time columns (listed_time, expiry…) are milliseconds since 1970: pd.to_datetime(col, unit="ms").
  • Push only a small cleaned file to GitHub (GitHub refuses files over 100 MB).
jobs = pd.read_csv("postings.csv", usecols=lambda c: c not in ("description", "skills_desc"))
skills = pd.read_csv("jobs/job_skills.csv").merge(pd.read_csv("mappings/skills.csv"), on="skill_abr")
jobs["listed"] = pd.to_datetime(jobs["listed_time"], unit="ms")

Watch out for

These are hints, not instructions. Check each one in the data, decide what to do, and explain your choice in the notebook.
  • Only about 30% of postings give a salary, and in different pay periods: hourly, weekly, monthly, yearly. Turn them into one unit before you compare.
  • Look at the salaries marked BIWEEKLY. Do they look like two weeks of pay?
  • The ready-made normalized_salary column has mistakes. Check it before you trust it.
  • The same job is sometimes posted twice.
  • Job titles are written in many ways (Sales Manager, sales manager, Sales Manager - Retail).
  • remote_allowed is 1 or empty: what does empty mean?
  • A posting can have several job functions and industries. Be careful not to count a posting twice.

Questions to explore

Demand

  1. Which job titles, job functions and industries have the most postings?

Work

  1. How many jobs are full-time, contract, part-time? Entry level or senior? Remote?
  2. Which kinds of jobs are most often remote?

Pay

  1. What does pay depend on: experience level, job function, title?
  2. Do remote jobs pay more?

Where and attention

  1. Which states have the most jobs, and the best pay?
  2. Which postings get the most views and applications?

Ideas for the app

  • A job explorer: filter by function, level, remote, state, or search a title.
  • A salary page built on one yearly salary unit.
  • A map of postings and median pay by state.
