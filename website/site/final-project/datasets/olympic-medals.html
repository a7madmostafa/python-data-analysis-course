The data

One row for every athlete in every event of the modern Olympic Games, Summer and Winter, from Athens 1896 to Rio 2016. It has the athlete's name, sex, age, height, weight, team, country code, the Games, sport, event, and the medal if they won one.

A second small file turns the three-letter country codes (EGY, USA, URS…) into country names.

About the format

  • The zip has two files: athlete_events.csv (the athletes) and noc_regions.csv (country code → country). You need to merge them on NOC.
  • One row = one athlete in one event. A swimmer in 5 events appears 5 times.
athletes = pd.read_csv("athlete_events.csv")
regions = pd.read_csv("noc_regions.csv")
df = athletes.merge(regions, on="NOC", how="left")

Watch out for

These are hints, not instructions. Check each one in the data, decide what to do, and explain your choice in the notebook.
  • Medal is empty for 85% of rows. Is that missing data, or something else?
  • ⚠️ Team medals: a football gold gives a row to each of the 18 players, but the country wins one medal. Count carefully.
  • Height and weight are missing for many early athletes.
  • Some codes are missing from noc_regions.csv, and old countries need decisions: the Soviet Union, East/West Germany, and the 1960 United Arab Republic (Egypt + Syria).
  • There are duplicate rows, and some events are not normal sports (the 1906 Games, art competitions).
  • The data stops in 2016: no Tokyo 2020 or Paris 2024.
